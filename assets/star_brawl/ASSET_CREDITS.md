# Art and sound

BioSphere X bundles CC0 assets by Kenney:

- [New Platformer Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/new-platformer-pack): fighter sprites, stars, gems, and sound effects
- [Platformer Pack Industrial](https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-pack-industrial): stage platforms
- [Planets](https://kenney.nl/assets/planets): planet artwork
- [Alien UFO Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/alien-ufo-pack): blaster effects
- [UI Pack Sci-Fi](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-sci-fi): interface artwork
- [Monster Builder Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/monster-builder-pack): bodies, limbs, faces, and head details assembled into ten boss characters

[Kenney's license details](https://kenney.nl/support) confirm these asset packs are public domain (CC0). The files are bundled for offline play.
