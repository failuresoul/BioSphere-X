# Orbit Arena 3D assets

The GLB models in this folder come from Kenney's [Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit), [Modular Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-space-kit), and [Blaster Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit). Kenney releases these packs under CC0 1.0. See the [Kenney support page](https://kenney.nl/support) for license details. The models were downloaded on 2026-10-22 and are bundled so the game runs offline.
